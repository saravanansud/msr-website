04 How It Works

We walk your floor first. We sell second.

The MSR sales motion. Five stages, every account, every time. Select a step to see what happens in it.

01 · Discovery — we walk your floor.

Site visit, route mapping, shift analysis. We look at where the work is, who is doing it, and what is slipping. No quote in the first meeting. The output is a one-page proposal that says yes-and-here-is-where, or an honest no.

02 · Proof of Concept — one day, free.

We bring a robot in for the day. Your staff sees it work, your operators ask the hard questions, your IT team sees the integration. Industry-validated 80% same-day close rate when the POC happens. We are not here to demo, we are here to prove.

03 · Paid Pilot — 30, 60, or 90 days.

One or two routes, real KPIs, real shifts. We tune the routing, we train the staff, we hand the data back at the end of the pilot. The customer decides whether it scales. The pilot pays for itself before commitment.

04 · Commitment — 3, 4, or 5 years.

Three ways to commit. RaaS, the subscription, half of every deployment. Capital lease, thirty percent. Outright purchase, twenty percent, often paired with the WEDC $35K robotics adoption grant and OBBBA 100% bonus depreciation. You pick what fits your balance sheet.

05 · Service & Scale — local, always.

One number for fleet support, firmware, route changes, new staff training, expansion to the next building. MSR runs your AMR fleet the way an MSP runs your IT. Local partner. Real names. Quarterly business reviews.

07 How You Pay

Three ways to commit. Pick the one that fits your balance sheet.

At launch, every MSR deployment will land as RaaS, capital lease, or outright purchase. The right answer depends on your finance team, not on us.

Robot as a Service. 50% of every deployment.

Predictable monthly subscription. We deliver the robot, we deploy it, we service it, we replace it when it ages out. Operating expense, not capital. Cancel after the first contract term, no salvage discussion.

  • Single monthly invoice covers hardware, software, service, training, replacement.
  • Scale up or down as your routes change quarter over quarter.
  • The flywheel half of our model. Cleaning-led customers default here.
  • Operating expense treatment. Zero capital approval required.
